kridge-lol

kridge-lol is a small, non-commercial bot that posts League of Legends patch notes and champion win rates to the League of Legends space on Kridge, a Japanese community site.

What it does

  • Checks Data Dragon daily for a new patch and posts the stat changes.
  • Collects ranked Solo/Duo matches from the JP and KR servers, played by Emerald+ players, and aggregates wins and games per patch × champion × role.
  • Every other day, posts one table per role (JP and KR side by side). Champions with fewer than 100 games are left out.

Riot Games API usage

  • league-v4: Master+ leagues and one page per Diamond/Emerald division, to pick players.
  • match-v5: match IDs and match details for those players, filtered to the current patch.
  • Runs once a day on GitHub Actions and stays within the key's rate limits.

Data and privacy

  • Only aggregate counts (games and wins per champion and role) are stored and published.
  • No summoner names, Riot IDs, PUUIDs or other personal data are stored or published. The only per-match data kept is the list of match IDs already counted, to avoid counting a match twice.
  • No login, no accounts, no ads, no payments.
